Privacy Policy

Pre-launch website policy · Last updated 23 October 2026

This policy currently applies only to the public RunForge informational website. The RunForge mobile app and Garmin integration are in development and are not available through this site. A fuller policy will be published before the app processes personal information or connects to Garmin.

About this website

RunForge is an independent running training project in development. This site provides information about planned features. It currently has no registration, login, newsletter, contact form, payment processing or activity upload features.

Information collected

We do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, fitness activities, location histories or Garmin account information through this website. We do not use advertising pixels or install our own analytics cookies. GitHub Pages, our hosting provider, may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs when serving the site, according to GitHub's own policies.

Cookies and third parties

This site does not deliberately set tracking cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. For details about GitHub's processing, see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Garmin integration

Garmin connectivity is a proposed future feature and is not active on this website. If approved and implemented, the integration is intended to work only with a runner's authorisation and to support transfer of workouts and completed activity data. Before any Garmin data is processed, this policy will be updated to describe data categories, purposes, legal bases, recipients, retention, deletion and account-disconnection rights. No Garmin data is currently collected by this website.

Data retention and your rights

Because the current website has no user accounts or submission forms, we do not maintain a website user database. Hosting-related records are handled by the hosting provider. When RunForge begins collecting personal data directly, we will provide information about applicable rights, including access, correction and deletion, and how to exercise them.

Contact and data controller

RunForge is a project name, not a representation that a limited company has been incorporated. The operator's business contact details will be published before any personal data collection through the RunForge service. Until those details are added, this page is a preliminary pre-launch policy and should not be represented as the final privacy policy for an operational app.

Changes

We will update this policy when the website or app gains new functionality. The last-updated date at the top will change when material revisions are made.
